### 兼职数据收集专员（1-2周项目）

**项目概述：**
我们正在寻找一名细心的自由职业者，协助为一家初创企业收集中国特定行业国内公司的背景信息。本项目为期1–2周，旨在建立一个初步的行业数据库。工作可通过人工完成，也可借助脚本工具。

**工作内容：**
1.  **目标识别：** 筛选并整理约 50-100 家目标公司（主要位于山东、浙江、广东）及其相关的 100-300 家企业实体。
2.  **国家企业信用信息公示系统 (GSXT) 查询：** 手动查询国家企业信用信息公示系统，以核实企业的登记状态、经营范围（需特别按特定行业进行筛选）、注册资本及主要股东信息。
3.  **股权穿透 (使用企查查/天眼查)：** 通过商业数据库查询并记录企业的**多层股权结构**，直到穿透至自然人或最终控制人。
4.  **合规性核查：** 记录企业的**行政处罚**、**经营异常名录**及法律诉讼记录。
5.  **地理位置验证：** 使用百度地图核实厂区的具体坐标和物理地址，确保实体真实存在而非壳公司。
6.  **数据归档：** 将收集到的所有文本信息、截图和文件准确输入到我们提供的**Excel电子表格**中。

**任职要求：**
*   具备极强的细节观察能力，能够准确分辨复杂的企业关联关系。
*   能够将中文专业术语（如经营范围）准确录入。
*   需要中国大陆手机号。

### Freelance Data Collection Specialist (1-2 Week Project: China Refinery & Compliance Data)

**Project Overview:**
We are seeking a detail-oriented freelancer to assist in manual data collection for a data-driven startup focused on business intelligence. This is a **1-2 week project** intended to bootstrap our database. The work may be done either manually or with scripting.

**Key Responsibilities:**
1.  **Entity Identification:** Identify and list approximately 50-100 target companies (primarily in Shandong, Zhejiang, and Guangdong) and their related 100-300 corporate entities.
2.  **GSXT Verification:** Manually query the **National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (GSXT)** to verify registration status, **Business Scope** (specifically confirming target sectors), **Registered Capital**, and top-level shareholders.
3.  **Ownership Penetration (QCC/Tianyancha):** Use commercial platforms to map out **multi-tier ownership chains** down to natural persons or ultimate beneficial owners.
4.  **Compliance Screening:** Record any administrative penalties, abnormal operations, and legal litigation history.
5.  **Physical Verification:** Use Baidu Maps to verify refinery coordinates and physical addresses to ensure the facility exists and is not a shell entity.
6.  **Data Organization:** Enter all collected text data, screenshots, and supporting information into a structured **Excel spreadsheet**.

**Requirements:**
*   Strong attention to detail with the ability to accurately trace complex corporate relationships.
*   Ability to accurately record and organize technical Chinese business terminology.
