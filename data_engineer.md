# Job Description: 
# Data and Automation Engineer

## 1. Role Summary
We are seeking a pragmatic Data Automation Engineer. In this role, you will own the end-to-end process of acquiring external data at scale, to automating workflows that deliver clean, structured data to our internal systems and agents. Your work will directly impact decision-making by ensuring reliable access to critical commercial data from a variety of internal and third-party sources. 

## 2. Key Responsibilities
*   **Data Extraction (~30%):**
    *   Design, develop, and maintain robust, scalable data pipelines. Data sources are internal and external and include both real-time feeds and batch load. Data is both structured and unstructured.
    *   Parse and extract structured data from HTML, JS-rendered pages (using headless browsers), APIs, PDFs, and other unstructured formats.
    *   Ensure data quality and reliability by building comprehensive monitoring, logging, and alerting for data freshness.

*   **Workflow Automation & Orchestration (~70%):**
    *   Use OpenClaw or similar harness as the primary orchestration layer to define, schedule, and manage chat-based and automated agentic workflows.
    *   Build automation pipelines that integrate databases, data warehouses, internal APIs, and reporting tools.
    *   Implement fault-tolerant workflow logic, including error handling, retries, conditional branching, and graceful failure notifications.
    *   Collaborate with data analysts to understand requirements and automate manual data-fetching and processing tasks, improving operational efficiency.
    *   Document data schemas, data dictionaries, pipeline designs, and operational manuals for all owned systems.

## 3. Required Skills & Experience
*   1-5 years of hands-on experience in a data engineering or automation-focused role.
*   **Hands-on experience with OpenClaw, Hermes Agent or a similar RPA/workflow orchestration tool** (e.g., Apache Airflow, n8n, UI.Vision) to build, schedule, and monitor automated tasks.
*   In-depth, practical familiarity with the **Chinese web ecosystem**, including the structure and access patterns of major platforms.
*   Experience with data storage and pipelining using SQL databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL) and cloud storage (e.g., S3, OSS).
*   Experience with containerization (Docker) in containerized/cloud environments.
*   Ability to **read and write** English for communication with stakeholders is required.

## 4. Nice-to-Have Skills
*   Knowledge of proxy service providers.
*   Spoken English is nice to have but not required. 
*   Experience in a startup or SME environment, comfortable with high ownership and pragmatic problem-solving.

## 5. Tools & Stack
*   **Primary Languages:** Python/Go
*   **Scraping & Browser Automation:** Playwright, Puppeteer, Selenium, BeautifulSoup, Scrapy
*   **Workflow Orchestration:** OpenClaw, Apache Airflow
*   **Data Storage:** PostgreSQL, S3 or similar / Alibaba Cloud OSS
*   **Infrastructure:** Docker, Git, Linux
*   **Proxy & Infrastructure Services:** Various residential/data center proxy networks
