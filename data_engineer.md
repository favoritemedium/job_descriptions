# Job Description: 
# Data and Automation Engineer

## 1. Role Summary
We are seeking a pragmatic Scraping & Automation Data Engineer to build and maintain the data pipelines that fuel our business intelligence. In this role, you will own the end-to-end process of acquiring external data at scale, from designing resilient scrapers for complex Chinese web sources to automating workflows that deliver clean, structured data to our internal systems. Your work will directly impact decision-making by ensuring reliable access to critical commercial data from company registries, e-commerce platforms, and regulatory sources. You will balance technical execution with an understanding of the legal and operational realities of data extraction in China.

## 2. Key Responsibilities
*   **Scraping & Data Extraction (~60%):**
    *   Design, develop, and maintain robust, scalable web scraping pipelines for a variety of Chinese data sources (e.g., Tianyancha, procurement platforms).
    *   Implement and evolve strategies to overcome anti-scraping measures, including proxy/IP rotation, request throttling, browser fingerprint management, and optical barrier handling.
    *   Parse and extract structured data from HTML, JS-rendered pages (using headless browsers), APIs, PDFs, and other unstructured formats.
    *   Ensure data quality and reliability by building comprehensive monitoring, logging, and alerting for scraping health and data freshness.
    *   Stay updated on the technical and legal landscape of web scraping in China, ensuring all activities operate within the boundaries of PIPL, DSL, and platform Terms of Service.

*   **Workflow Automation & Orchestration (~40%):**
    *   Use OpenClaw or similar harness as the primary orchestration layer to define, schedule, and manage complex, multi-step data extraction and automation workflows.
    *   Build automation pipelines that integrate scraped data with downstream systems, such as databases, data warehouses, internal APIs, and dashboarding tools.
    *   Implement fault-tolerant workflow logic, including error handling, retries, conditional branching, and graceful failure notifications.
    *   Collaborate with data analysts and product teams to understand requirements and automate manual data-fetching and processing tasks, improving operational efficiency.
    *   Document data schemas, data dictionaries, pipeline designs, and operational manuals for all owned systems.

## 3. Required Skills & Experience
*   1-5 years of hands-on experience in a data engineering, scraping, or automation-focused role.
*   **Proficient in Python or Go** for data scraping and automation, with strong knowledge of libraries like Playwright/Selenium, BeautifulSoup, lxml, and requests.
*   **Proven experience with web scraping at scale:** deep understanding of HTTP/HTTPS, sessions, cookies, browser developer tools, and modern anti-bot evasion techniques.
*   **Hands-on experience with OpenClaw or a similar RPA/workflow orchestration tool** (e.g., Apache Airflow, n8n, UI.Vision) to build, schedule, and monitor automated tasks.
*   In-depth, practical familiarity with the **Chinese web ecosystem**, including the structure and access patterns of major platforms, Baidu search behaviors, and common regional anti-scraping technologies.
*   Experience with data storage and pipelining using SQL databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL) and cloud storage (e.g., S3, OSS).
*   Experience with containerization (Docker) and running scrapers in containerized/cloud environments.
*   Understanding of relevant Chinese data regulations (PIPL, DSL) and a strong sense of ethical scraping practices.
*   Ability to read and write English for communication with stakeholders is required.

## 4. Nice-to-Have Skills
*   Knowledge of proxy service providers and management platforms commonly used in China.
*   Spoken English is nice to have but not required. 
*   Experience in a startup or SME environment, comfortable with high ownership and pragmatic problem-solving.

## 5. Tools & Stack
*   **Primary Languages:** Python/Go
*   **Scraping & Browser Automation:** Playwright, Puppeteer, Selenium, BeautifulSoup, Scrapy
*   **Workflow Orchestration:** **OpenClaw**, Apache Airflow
*   **Data Storage:** PostgreSQL, S3 or similar / Alibaba Cloud OSS
*   **Infrastructure:** Docker, Git, Linux
*   **Proxy & Infrastructure Services:** Various residential/data center proxy networks
